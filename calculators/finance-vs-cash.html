How it's calculated

Finance path: pay the down payment, invest the rest of the purchase price as a lump sum, and make the loan payments from your monthly cash flow. Net worth is the investment balance minus whatever is still owed on the loan.

Cash path: pay the full price up front, then invest the same monthly payment amount for the length of the loan. After the loan term ends, both paths just compound.

Returns compound monthly at the annual rate you enter. No taxes. The break-even return is the loan's effective annual rate: above it, financing wins.
