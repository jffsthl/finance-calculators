How the numbers are built

Eligibility, strictest setting

  • SEP: age 21, worked in 3 of the last 5 years, paid at least the SEP minimum this year. Covered employees get the same % of pay as the owner.
  • SIMPLE IRA: earned $5,000+ in any 2 prior years and expected to earn $5,000+ this year. No age exclusion is allowed.
  • 401(k): age 21 and one year with 1,000+ hours, entering on the next Jan 1 or Jul 1. Someone entering Jul 1 counts half-year pay.
  • Long-term part-time (LTPT): 500–999 hours for 2 straight years. They may defer, but no safe harbor or profit sharing is owed. They still end Solo 401(k) status.

Owner compensation

Self-employed: net profit minus staff contributions, minus half of SE tax, then the 25% employer rate works out to 20% of that figure. SIMPLE uses 92.35% of net profit (Schedule SE line 4). S-corp: W-2 wages, 25% flat. Pay above the compensation cap is ignored.

Order the owner fills a plan

SIMPLE and 401(k) fill elective deferrals first, then catch-up, then employer money. In the safe harbor plan, profit sharing is pro-rata (same % of pay for everyone). A cross-tested or new comparability design could lower staff cost; that needs a TPA's testing.

Tax savings

Deductible dollars × (federal + state marginal rate). For Schedule C owners, staff contributions also lower SE tax, which is counted. Not counted: the drop in the QBI (§199A) deduction, the 0.9% additional Medicare tax, SECURE 2.0 startup and employer-contribution credits, and the auto-enrollment credit.

Roth catch-up rule

S-corp owners whose prior-year FICA wages were above the threshold must make 401(k) catch-ups as Roth (assumes last year's wages match this year's). Doesn't apply to SEP, SIMPLE IRA, or Schedule C income.

Other rules to check

  • A new SIMPLE IRA for the year must be set up by Oct 1 (later only for a business started after that). It must be the only plan that year, and withdrawals in the first 2 years carry a 25% penalty.
  • SIMPLE employers with 25 or fewer $5,000+ earners get the higher SECURE 2.0 deferral limit automatically.
  • Solo 401(k): Form 5500-EZ once assets pass $250,000.
  • Controlled-group and affiliated-service-group rules, top-heavy minimums, and 2-year eligibility for employer money aren't modeled.
