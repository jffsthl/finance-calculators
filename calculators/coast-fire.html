How this works

Your FIRE number is your annual cost of living, inflated to your retirement age, divided by the safe withdrawal rate. The coast number is the balance you need at your coast age so that, growing with no further contributions, it reaches the FIRE number at retirement.

The monthly rate is the effective-annual conversion (1 + annual)1/12 − 1. Contributions are made at the end of each month until the coast age. Today's-dollar figures divide by inflation from today.

For education only, not financial, tax or investment advice. Real returns and inflation vary, and the order of returns matters. Taxes, fees and Social Security are not included.
