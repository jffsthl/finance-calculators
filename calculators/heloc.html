How it works

  • Rate. Prime + your margin, limited by the floor and lifetime cap, set each month from the rate path. Interest is balance × rate ÷ 12 each month (lenders usually accrue daily, so real statements differ by a few dollars).
  • Minimum payment. Interest-only draw: the month's interest. P&I draw: the payment that would pay off the balance over the draw months left plus the repayment period. Repayment: the payment that pays off the balance over the repayment months left. Both are recalculated every month at that month's rate, so a rate change moves the payment right away.
  • Your plans. Current plan = the larger of your monthly payment and the minimum. Extra plan = the current plan's payment rule plus your extra each month and the lump sums (applied in the month shown, after that month's payment). No new draws are assumed.
  • Payment shock compares the minimum due in the last draw-period month with the first repayment month.

Estimates only. Your lender's statement, rounding, fees, daily-accrual method and any fixed-rate locks will change the numbers.
