Trump-Sons Board & Advisory Role Backtest

An equal-weight portfolio of listed companies where Donald Trump Jr. or Eric Trump holds a board, advisory or (for American Bitcoin) executive role, bought once the role became public. Daily data, research backtest.

Portfolio

Small sample. MAIN is 7 names over about 2 years. PLUS FAMILY's first 2½ years (Apr 2022 – Nov 2024) are DJT alone. Results are driven by a few very volatile microcaps; treat every statistic as descriptive.

Method

Holdings. MAIN: Unusual Machines (UMAC, from Nov 27, 2024), PublicSquare (PSQH, Dec 3, 2024), GrabAGun (held as Colombier Acquisition Corp. II from the Jan 6, 2025 advisor announcement, then PEW after the July 2025 merger), Dominari (DOMH, Feb 11, 2025; one position although both brothers are advisors), Metaplanet (3350.T in Tokyo, Mar 21, 2025, converted to dollars), ALT5 Sigma (ALTS, renamed AIFC, Aug 11, 2025) and Mixed Martial Arts Group (MMA, Sep 9, 2025). PLUS FAMILY adds Trump Media (held as Digital World Acquisition Corp. from Apr 25, 2022, then DJT) and American Bitcoin (held as Gryphon Digital Mining from the May 12, 2025 merger announcement, then ABTC; Eric Trump's role there is executive, not board). PLUS SPACs adds New America Acquisition I (NWAX), Colombier Acquisition Corp. III (CLBR) and McKinley Acquisition (MKLY, from Jul 31, 2026).

Rules. Equal weight across active holdings, rebalanced to equal whenever a name is added or removed and at every month-end. The buy-and-hold version gives a new name an equal share of the portfolio's current value, funded pro rata from the existing holdings, and never rebalances otherwise. Positions are sold on the role end date; only ALT5 Sigma has one. Returns are daily, close to close, using split- and dividend-adjusted prices.

Entry prices. (a) the announcement-day close. (b) the next day's open. (c) the announcement-day open. UMAC, PSQH, Colombier II, DOMH, Gryphon, ALTS and MMA were treated as pre-market announcements: each opened with a large gap on heavy volume, and Dominari's 8:30am ET release is documented. For those, (c) buys after the overnight gap, so it does not capture the pop. Metaplanet announced during the Tokyo session (open +2.6%, close +18%), so (c) does capture it. The timing for DWAC and MKLY is unknown, so (c) uses the day-0 open. NWAX and Colombier III announced the roles before listing, so they enter at their first available share trade.

Costs. The cost run charges 50bp on every dollar traded (entries, exits and rebalancing) in microcaps, defined as a market value under about $300M at entry (approximate, from public reporting): UMAC, PSQH, Colombier II/PEW, DOMH, ALTS, MMA, Gryphon/ABTC and the three SPACs. DJT and Metaplanet are not charged. Real spreads on the smallest names are likely wider.

Statistics. Sharpe ratios use arithmetic daily excess returns over 3-month T-bills (FRED DTB3). At about 100% volatility the drag from volatility is roughly 50% a year, so a Sharpe near zero can sit alongside an 80% loss. Betas are daily regressions on IWM and on bitcoin.

Caveats

  • Sample size. 7 names over about 2 years in MAIN; 9 in PLUS FAMILY, where Apr 2022 – Nov 2024 is DJT alone. One or two names move the whole result (see leave-one-out).
  • UMAC concentration. UMAC was the only holding from its Nov 27, 2024 entry until PSQH was added on Dec 3, so the MAIN portfolio took UMAC's +89% on Nov 29, 2024 in full. That day is MAIN's high point.
  • Possible leaks. DOMH rose 91% on Feb 10, 2025, the day before the 8:30am Feb 11 release (the advisory agreement is dated Feb 10). Gryphon and ALTS also ran up in the five days before their announcements (abnormal returns of about +68% and +36%). None of this is tradable on public information.
  • PSQH. The NYSE suspended PSQH on Nov 9, 2026 and began delisting proceedings; it now trades on OTC Pink. Prices after that date are OTC quotes.
  • ALT5 Sigma exit date is unverified. Eric Trump was removed from the company's leadership page around April 2026 per press reports; no 8-K was found. The "still held" option shows the alternative.
  • DWAC event date. The Apr 25, 2022 date is press coverage of an Apr 18, 2022 Florida corporate filing that listed Don Jr. as a TMTG director, so day 0 is not a clean announcement.
  • SPACs. SPAC units and warrants are ignored; only common shares are held. NWAX units (Dec 2025 – Jan 2026) and Colombier III units (from Feb 4, 2026) are not in the data, so those names enter when their shares began trading.
